Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

onurhelps.com is a personal, free website offering interactive engineering tools for mechanical and structural design engineers. It's operated independently by Onur Surlu. This page explains what happens with data when you visit.

What this site does not do

onurhelps.com has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no contact or feedback forms that collect personal information. None of the calculators store your inputs on any server tied to your identity, and no cookies are set directly by this site's own code.

The calculation tools

Two tools — Bolt Pretension / External Load, and the Rigid Body Reaction Force Solver — send the numeric engineering values you enter (dimensions, forces, material properties, and similar) to a private calculation server so the result can be computed, then display the result back to you. These requests carry only the numeric inputs needed for the calculation, contain no personal information, and are not stored or associated with any visitor identity. The other two tools (Mohr's Circle and Monte Carlo Tolerance Calculation) run entirely in your own browser and never send your inputs anywhere.

Advertising & cookies

This site shows advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick/Google Ads cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. This site does not control what these third-party cookies do beyond what's described in Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings — to see and adjust the ad personalization Google shows you
  • aboutads.info — to opt out of personalized advertising from participating vendors generally

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent message (via a Google-certified consent management platform) before any personalized advertising cookies are set, and can change their choice at any time through that message.

Analytics

This site does not currently use Google Analytics or any other visitor-tracking/analytics service beyond the advertising cookies described above.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site or the advertising it carries changes. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
